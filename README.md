# 🏈 Grid Iron

A prime-time, broadcast-style football sim you play in the browser — built mobile-first and installable to your Home Screen.

**▶ Play:** https://dianteclark.github.io/Grid-Iron/

## What's in it
- **Home field (v0.7)**: all 32 teams have a stadium — dome, retractable roof or open air; grass, turf or blue turf; a climate that drives the weather (snow up north late in the year, heat down south early). Turf is fast, wet grass is sloppy, thin air carries kicks, domes are loud. The home team paints the end zones, crowds fill in with hype, and the Iron-Bowl is played at a neutral site. Renovate and rename your stadium in franchise mode.
- **Quick game setup**: Home / Away / Neutral / 🎲 venue, Auto / 🎲 / pick-your-weather, random team or opponent, and a one-tap 🎲 Surprise Me. Rematch, swap sides, share the result, and track your record with every team.
- **Store, full circle**: jerseys are set per team and cut from that team's colors (Color Rush, Heritage unlocked by finishing a team's album page), your field skin shows at your home stadium, plus game balls and scorebug themes. First win of the day pays +5 Legacy Points.
- **Broadcast polish**: linescore and game-flow chart, drive summaries, red-zone glow, sideline chains and down marker, camera flashes in the stands, snow cover and rain sheen, keys to the game, keyboard shortcuts, phone back-gesture support, reduce-motion and high-contrast options.
- **Prime-time look (v0.6)**: night-game stadium with a confetti crowd and ad boards, mown checkerboard turf, glossy 3D player pieces with direction notches, a broadcast name tag on the ball carrier, dashed run lines, a chunky scorebug, and a bottom tab bar (Pause, Speed, Wide camera, Tactics, Stats, Key moments). New app icon.
- **Career Mode**: build a player (position, college, number, attributes, a signature trait), star in 4 college games where you control his key moments on the field, run the combine, read the mock drafts, then enter your franchise's draft or go pro and play a whole career through awards, contracts and the Hall of Fame.
- **League life**: rivalry heat, coaching trees (revenge games, coaches who know each other's playbooks), stars facing the team that cut them, QB–receiver duos, star rivalries, playbook identities, mentors, traits, playing hurt, holdouts and hot-seat storylines that carry across weeks, press conferences, power rankings and league news.
- **Front office**: salary cap, free-agency bidding wars, trades for stars and picks, a coaching market (retired stars become coaches), the owner's goals and yearly review (you can get fired), fan hype, hometown heroes, a draft lottery and dynasty difficulty.
- **Legacy**: awards night (MVP, OPOY, DPOY, ROY, Coach of the Year, the All-Iron team), a record book, a Hall of Fame, Legacy Points, a store with jerseys, field skins and touchdown celebrations, a sticker album, a season highlight reel and a collector's-edition championship Gazette.
- **Game day depth**: down & distance matters, opponent tendencies on the play sheet, a "what they called" reveal after every snap, timeouts, hurry-up, spike, kneel and the two-minute warning, momentum, halftime adjustments, key-moments mode, sim a drive or a half, slow-motion replays, auto-arm, record and milestone banners, first-game coach tips.
- **Backups**: download a backup file or copy a code from Settings and restore it on any device.
- **Franchise mode**: every team starts empty. A 4-round opening draft (K/P, QB and LB required in rounds 1–3) leaves teams with 3–5 stars and room to grow; later drafts grow to 5, 6, 7 and 8 rounds. Prospects show scouted rating ranges (fuzzy until you draft them), potential grades and ⚡ breakthrough spark. Auto-pick and auto-draft included.
- **Contracts & budget**: rookie deals, salaries, re-signing or letting stars walk, one backup star whose contract only starts when he plays, ticket and TV revenue, an Iron-Bowl purse for the next draft.
- **Spring training** with camp focus, intense camps and rookie breakthroughs (+8 to +15).
- **Front office upgrades**: stadium, gym, scouts and medical.
- **Drama**: trade demands from big egos, coordinators poached by rivals, coaches on the hot seat, scout reports on prospects, local walk-on legends.
- **32 team crests** with unique mascot emblems; stars are gold-ringed pieces wearing their numbers.
- **Seasons**: 8, 12 or 17-week seasons across two conferences (Iron & Steel) and eight divisions, full standings, a 12-team playoff and the **Iron-Bowl**. Injuries carry across weeks, morale rides streaks, and every other game in the league is simulated with the same engine.
- **The Grid-Iron Gazette**: a full-screen newspaper after every week with your headline, a play-of-the-week photo, injury report, scores, your division, and an EXTRA! story that can change your next game (leaked game plans, film study, storms, sellouts) or make you choose how to handle a star's ego.
- **32 custom teams**, each with **8 star slots**: every team has a K/P, QB and LB star, plus 5 flexible slots (QB, RB, WR, TE, OL, LB, S, DL, CB — at least one more on offense and one on defense), so every roster has its own identity.
- **Call every snap** on offense and defense: Inside Run, Outside Run, Short Pass, Long Pass, Trick Play vs Man, Zone, Cover Long, Press, Blitz In, Blitz Out. Every play has one hard counter (see the Counter Chart in game).
- **Stars stand out on the field.** Their Show-Me meter fills by making plays; at 100% they catch fire 🔥. Arm them for a Strip Sack, Pick Six, Lockdown, Breakaway, Pancake Block (+15), Circus Catch, Dime Drop or Big Leg kick. Two bad plays while on fire cools them off. You can see the other team's stars on fire too.
- **Star clashes**: when stars on both sides go off on the same snap, ratings, morale, matchup and weather decide who wins.
- **Play of the Week**: one offensive and one defensive play get a boost for 3 reps a game. The CPU has its own. Calling the same play 3 snaps in a row gets you read.
- **Star rail**: all 8 stars always on screen; tap a ring to see the star and arm him any time, even during a kickoff.
- **Game day**: weather (rain, snow, wind, heat), crowd noise (false starts, home meter boost), injuries, coordinators, morale, a CPU that adapts if you spam a play.
- Full-screen animated field (runs top-to-bottom in portrait, left-to-right in landscape) with kickoffs, punts, field goals, 2-point tries and overtime.
- Synthesized sound effects, crowd noise and menu music, plus Settings with sound toggles, theme and a full data reset.
- Auto-saves every snap; resume anytime. Works offline once loaded.

## Add to Home Screen
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → *Install app* / *Add to Home screen*
