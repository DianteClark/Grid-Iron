# 🏈 Grid Iron

A sticker-style football sim you play in the browser — built mobile-first and installable to your Home Screen.

**▶ Play:** https://dianteclark.github.io/Grid-Iron/

## Phase 1 (this version)
- **32 custom teams**, each with **8 star slots**: every team has a K/P, QB and LB star, plus 5 flexible slots (at least one more on offense and one on defense), so every roster has its own identity.
- **Call every snap** on offense and defense: Inside Run, Outside Run, Short Pass, Long Pass, Trick Play vs Man, Zone, Cover Long, Press, Blitz In, Blitz Out. Every play has one hard counter (see the Counter Chart in game).
- **Show-Me meters**: stars fill their meter by making plays. At 100%, tap to arm them for a Strip Sack, Pick Six, Breakaway, Pancake Block (+15), Circus Catch, Dime Drop or Big Leg kick.
- **Play of the Week**: your coach drills one offensive and one defensive play for a boost.
- **Game day**: weather (rain, snow, wind, heat), crowd noise (false starts, home meter boost), injuries, coordinators, morale, a CPU that adapts if you spam a play.
- Animated top-down field with circle players, kickoffs, punts, field goals, 2-point tries and overtime.
- Auto-saves every snap; resume anytime. Works offline once loaded.

## Add to Home Screen
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → *Install app* / *Add to Home screen*

## Run locally
Any static server works, e.g. `python3 -m http.server` and open `http://localhost:8000`.
